The two-tier structure means the platform is open enough to attract a developer ecosystem while keeping every piece of strategic IP — the STAR WEB5 engine, the WEB6 AI orchestration layer, the node infrastructure, the game engine bridge, and the IDE — firmly under proprietary control. Community developers depend on and build trust in the public surface; the private core is what they cannot replicate without years of dedicated engineering effort. This structure directly protects defensibility while maximising adoption velocity.
