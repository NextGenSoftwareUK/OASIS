## Original price binding — implemented 2026-10-17 (Core44f0ae4)

`NativeFeePriceQuote` and `NativePreparationPersistenceWithPrices` are central Core facilities, not provider-local billing engines. They retain the original service-owned price with signed preparation before submission. Existing unpriced callback signature is preserved and delegates to the same implementation.

- The quote binds operation UUID, provider, chain/network, exact denomination, market asset ID, decimal scale, positive USD/whole-unit rate, source/version, catalogue version, and UTC publish/fetch/bind/valid-until times. There are no inferred gas assets/scales, default quote lifetimes or embedded credentials. The trusted host must authorize the catalogue, source, acceptable observation age and admission window; ordered timestamps alone do not prove source authority or freshness policy.
- `BindObservation` reuses the existing immutable `AssetUsdQuote` without another fetch and rejects an asset-ID mismatch. It does not approve arbitrary caller-supplied price data. Quote validity is checked at attempt admission; operation/provider/chain mismatches, expired/future admission and duplicate denominations reject before persistence/broadcast. Up to32 unique denomination quotes per preparation are included in the existing8MiB operation evidence budget.
- Full price evidence is retained atomically with original signed bytes; callback and store snapshot caller-owned arrays/envelopes. Mongo uses explicit UUID, Decimal128 price/cost and string-encoded UTC timestamps to preserve precision through restart. Existing no-TTL outbox/idempotency retention preserves the quote audit reference.
- `ValueOriginalReceipt` uses central exact native-unit conversion, returns signed fee/rebate evidence and references `bound-native-v1:` plus the full quote SHA256 in the existing valuation-version field. After an admitted operation's quote expires, recovery reuses that original price; it does not obtain today's price. Native receipt identity, denomination, payer and outcome must still be independently verified. This is an admission-price mechanism, not a native fee cap, markup, future-FX guarantee or spending authorization.
- Receipt append and settlement queue/recovery validate the valuation against the attempt's retained quote and exact operation cost. Replacing source/version/rate evidence, removing priced evidence or changing the total is rejected. The existing native settlement receipt schema/hashes are unchanged; full quote resides in the outbox and its fingerprint is bound by the existing receipt hash. WEB4 still trusts its authenticated consuming service's attestation; the quote fingerprint is not standalone oracle/signature proof.

## Approved billing policy — controlled accounting integrated; production acceptance pending

The user approved netting native fees and storage rebates **within the same admitted operation**, with a minimum final charge of zero. There is no payout, carry-forward credit, or offset against another operation.

- Retain every original signed attempt and its independently verified native receipt, including charged failures and negative net fees.
- Sum fees and rebates for the operation before applying the zero floor. Do not discard negative receipts or clamp each attempt separately.
- Example in one denomination: fees 10 and rebate 3 produce charge 7. Fees 3 and rebate 10 produce charge 0, not a payout of 7.
- Another operation's costs cannot absorb the unused rebate.
- Keep denominations explicit. MIST, SUN, tinybar and Cosmos denomination amounts are not interchangeable numbers. Mixed currencies require authoritative, auditable valuation before a combined monetary charge can be established.
- An unavailable receipt, unknown outcome, absent price or unsupported payer attribution is unresolved evidence—not zero usage and not permission to release the reservation.

This approval does not itself authorize public-chain spending, paid cloud provisioning, a production deployment, a particular exchange-rate source, or a subscription allowance change.

## Required acceptance before fee recovery is complete

1. Preserve original attempt identity, chain, signatures, payer and exact signed fee components.
2. Collect every physical attempt's terminal fee evidence, including failures and duplicates where charged.
3. Retain signed rebates until all same-operation evidence is aggregated; floor only the final charge.
4. Bind valuation source, rate/time, denomination and rounding policy to durable evidence. Missing valuation stays unresolved.
5. Prove idempotent settlement, restart recovery, concurrent-worker behavior and no cross-operation rebate leakage.
6. Prove actual host registration/admission and authorized spending policy; local synthetic-authority tests alone do not prove this.
7. Document provider/network-specific exclusions and run the required native/hosted gates before merge.
