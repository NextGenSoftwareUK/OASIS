11 November Firebase versioning audit. The adapter has no persisted history for holons, avatars or avatar details. A version argument currently reaches a live-row query, including username, email, metadata and owned-export selectors. The existing ETag avatar deletion already demonstrates the native conditional-write entry point. Firebase REST permits conditional PUT and DELETE and does not provide conditional PATCH. A GET followed by a regular multi-row PATCH cannot guarantee that the archived row is the one the write replaced. The history design must make previous-row capture, immutable revision insertion and head publication one conditional native operation. Ordinary graph updates must also preserve their all-or-nothing selected-row write. Verify stale-writer rejection, historical identity and lookup values after rename, versioned tombstone visibility, restart persistence, legacy head access and exact cleanup on RTDB before counting history as accepted. [Firebase conditional writes](https://firebase.google.com/docs/database/rest/save-data#updating-data-with-conditional-requests)
